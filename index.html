About me

I am a software engineer with a background in artificial intelligence, robotics, and production Python systems. In my professional work, I build reliable geospatial and Earth-observation pipelines that turn satellite, terrain, and weather data into practical environmental and agricultural services. My experience spans data processing, machine learning, automation, testing, and containerized deployment.

I recently completed an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence, with a thesis on safety-constrained autonomous quadruped navigation in three-dimensional terrain. I also co-developed a winning LLM-driven ROS 2 mission workflow with task planning, reusable robot skills, and explicit safety constraints. I enjoy bringing research ideas into dependable real-world systems, especially where AI, perception, and autonomous robotics meet.
